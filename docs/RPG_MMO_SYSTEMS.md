# RPG + MMORPG Systems

## RPG
- Character classes and subclasses
- Attributes and combat statistics
- Levels, XP, skill points and talents
- Skills and cooldowns
- Equipment and inventory
- Items, rarity and loot
- Quests and objectives
- Achievements
- Buffs, debuffs and crowd control
- Threat and aggro
- Crafting and recipes
- Gold and premium currency
- Zones, dungeons, raids and bosses

## MMORPG
- Player sessions and online state
- Account/character persistence snapshots
- Parties and guilds
- Guild banks and progression data
- Mail and attachments
- Matchmaking queues
- Auction house
- World zones and spawn points
- Dungeon and raid definitions
- Server-authoritative combat foundation
- Deterministic simulation primitives

## Planned production services

The engine can be extended with authentication, database repositories, shard/realm management, chat, friends, guild permissions, anti-cheat validation, rate limiting, instance allocation, server discovery, replication, reconnect/resume, metrics, telemetry, and dedicated-server networking.
